Snowscooting in Les Deux Alpes

Where can I hire a snowscoot in Les Deux Alpes?

At Milou Sports, 126 avenue de la Muzelle, 38860 Les Deux Alpes, 200 m from the Diable chairlift and the lift pass offices. We hire new-generation full-suspension snowscoots from the Centsix brand.

New-generation, full-suspension Centsix snowscoots, available in several ranges. Our team will recommend the right model for your level and the kind of riding you want.

When can I hire a snowscoot at Milou Sports?

Throughout the winter season, from 27 November 2026 to 2 May 2027, every day from 8:30 am to 7:30 pm.
