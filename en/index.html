The Shop

Style at the summit

In a corner shop in the heart of the resort, just a short walk from the slopes, Milou Sports brings together the great names in luxury skiwear and mountain fashion in a warm wooden setting.

Every piece is chosen for its cut, its fabrics and how it performs on the slopes as well as in town. Technical ski suits, iconic down jackets, soft knitwear and après-ski outfits: a complete wardrobe for a season at altitude.

Our team of enthusiasts brings together more than 30 years of experience: a know-how that is increasingly rare, and our greatest strength. We welcome you in English and French, with attentive, unhurried advice.

L'Atelier by Milou

Your skis in expert hands

Because your skis deserve the best, our workshop prepares them by hand, like a collector's piece.

Open onto the street like a chef’s kitchen, the workshop is entirely dedicated to your equipment: every rental ski is serviced after each use, for a perfect glide every time.

Our ski technicians also take care of your own equipment: drop your skis off in the evening and, depending on how busy we are, pick them up ready to ride the next morning.

  • Hand hot waxing
  • Edge sharpening
  • Base repair
  • Binding adjustment
  • Servicing after each use
  • Open workshop, in full view

Waxing, step by step

A hot wax applied at the workbench, for a smooth glide and a base protected from the dry cold of the glacier.

  1. 1Melting the waxThe wax block melts against the iron.
  2. 2DrippingDrops are spread along the full length of the base.
  3. 3IroningThe iron spreads the wax so it soaks into the base.
  4. 4ScrapingOnce cooled, the excess is removed with a scraper.
  5. 5BrushingThe rotary brush opens up the structure for the glide.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you come

How do I book my ski hire in Les Deux Alpes?

Book online on our Sport 2000 Milou Sports booking page, by phone on +33 4 76 80 50 42, or directly in the shop.

Can I hire on the spot, without booking?

Yes. Thanks to our large rental fleet, you can come straight to the shop and leave fully equipped, without booking in advance.

Where can I find a luxury ski shop in Les Deux Alpes?

At Milou Sports, 126 avenue de la Muzelle: a luxury ski shop carrying Fusalp, Bogner, Perfect Moment, Colmar, Lacoste, From Future, UGG and Oakley, with premium ski hire and an open workshop in full view.

Where is the shop?

At 126 avenue de la Muzelle, 38860 Les Deux Alpes (Isère, France): an ideally located corner shop close to the slopes, 200 m from the Diable chairlift and the lift pass offices.

What are your opening hours?

Every day from 8:30 am to 7:30 pm during the winter season, from 27 November 2026 to 2 May 2027.

What equipment can I hire?

Skis (Blue, Red, Black and Diamond ranges), snowboards, snowscoots, ski boots, helmets and goggles, as well as equipment for children.

Do you hire snowscoots in Les Deux Alpes?

Yes: new-generation full-suspension Centsix snowscoots, in several ranges. All the details on our snowscoot hire in Les Deux Alpes page.

Can I change my equipment during my stay?

Yes, equipment can be exchanged during your stay. Bindings are adjusted to you and every pair of skis is serviced after each use in our open workshop, in full view from the street.

Can you service my own skis?

Yes, our workshop also services your own equipment: hot waxing, edge sharpening, base repair and binding adjustment. Drop your skis off in the evening and, depending on how busy we are, pick them up ready the next morning.

Do you offer ski storage?

Yes, we keep your skis at the shop, so you can head off light-hearted and relax after your day on the slopes.

Do you deliver skis to my accommodation?

Yes, ski delivery to your accommodation is possible. Call us on +33 4 76 80 50 42 to arrange it.

How much does ski hire cost?

Prices vary depending on your dates and the range you choose. Up-to-date prices are shown on our online booking page.

Which brands can I find in the shop?

Fusalp, Perfect Moment, Bogner, Colmar, Lacoste, From Future, UGG and Oakley (goggles, sunglasses and accessories). The pieces shown on this site are available in store only; they are not sold online.

Do you speak English?

Yes, our team welcomes you in English and French.
