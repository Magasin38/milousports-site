Frequently asked questions

Ski hire in Les 2 Alpes

Where can I hire skis in Les Deux Alpes?

At Milou Sports, 126 avenue de la Muzelle, 38860 Les Deux Alpes, 200 m from the Diable chairlift and the lift pass offices. Skis, snowboards, snowscoots, boots, helmets and kids' equipment, fitted to you.

How do I book ski hire in Les 2 Alpes?

Online on our Sport 2000 Milou Sports booking page, by phone on +33 4 76 80 50 42, or directly in the shop.

Can I hire on the spot, without booking?

Yes. Thanks to our large rental fleet, you can come straight to the shop and leave fully equipped, without booking in advance.

How much does ski hire cost?

Prices depend on your dates and the range you choose. You see the exact price in real time when you book online.

Which ski range should I choose?

Blue for beginners and occasional skiers, Red for intermediates, Black for advanced skiers, Diamond for the most demanding. In the shop, our team will advise you according to your level.

Do you hire snowboards in Les Deux Alpes?

Yes: freeride and all-mountain boards, with boots and bindings adjusted on site. For a different ride, try snowscoot hire.

Do you hire ski equipment for children?

Yes: skis, boots and helmets suited to every age, from the snow garden to the first red runs.

Can I change my equipment during my stay?

Yes, you can exchange your equipment during your stay.

Do you offer ski storage and delivery?

Yes. You can leave your skis at the shop overnight, and we can deliver your equipment to your accommodation: call +33 4 76 80 50 42 to arrange it.

What are the shop's opening hours?

Every day from 8:30 am to 7:30 pm during the winter season, from 27 November 2026 to 2 May 2027.
